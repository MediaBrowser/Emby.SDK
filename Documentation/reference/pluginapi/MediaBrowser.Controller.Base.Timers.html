    Namespace MediaBrowser.Controller.Base.Timers

    Classes

    IdleTimer

    A timer that tracks transitions between Busy, Idle and Suspended states based on activity signals and configurable timeouts.

    QuantizationTimerBase<TDataObject>

    QuantizationTimerDictionary<TKey, TValue>

    A dictionary of key/value pairs of types TKey and TValue that coalesces bursts of added or updated entries into batches, raising a single notification once no new changes have arrived for a configured idle delay (subject to a minimum interval between notifications).

    QuantizationTimerList<T>

    A list of items of type T that coalesces bursts of added items into batches, raising a single notification once no new items have arrived for a configured idle delay (subject to a minimum interval between notifications).

    QuantizationTimerQueue<T>

    A queue of items of type T that coalesces bursts of enqueued items into batches, raising a single notification once no new items have arrived for a configured idle delay (subject to a minimum interval between notifications).
